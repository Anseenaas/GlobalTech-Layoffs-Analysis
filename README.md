
# Global Tech Layoffs Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses global technology-sector layoffs from March 2020 to July 2025 using Microsoft Power BI. It explores layoff trends over time and compares layoffs across industries, companies, countries, and company stages.

## Objectives
- Analyse yearly and monthly layoff trends.
- Identify the most affected industries and companies.
- Compare layoffs across countries.
- Examine layoffs by company stage.
- Present key insights through interactive data visualisations.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total employees laid off: 752,040
- Total layoff events: 2,673
- Companies affected: 1,913
- Countries represented: 54
- Industries represented: 30

## Key Insights
- 2023 recorded the highest number of layoffs.
- January 2023 was the highest-layoff month highlighted in the analysis.
- The United States accounted for approximately 69% of layoffs.
- Post-IPO companies accounted for approximately 58% of layoffs.
- Hardware, Consumer, and Retail were among the most affected industries.
- The top 10 companies accounted for approximately 26% of total layoffs.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data analysis and visualisation
- KPI reporting
- Trend analysis

## Project Files
- `.pbix` — Power BI dashboard
- `.pptx` — Project presentation

## Limitations
- Funding amount data was unavailable.
- Company employee headcount was unavailable, so layoff percentages relative to total workforce could not be calculated.
- Data for 2025 covers only up to early July.

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights major patterns in technology-sector layoffs, including annual trends and differences across industries, companies, countries, and company stages. These findings provide a data-driven overview of layoff patterns during the period analysed.
